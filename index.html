02 · Why explore this now

The gaps are before and after the order, not at delivery

Feirm's project research found that delivery works well, while first contact and follow-up are where customers can be lost. Those are exactly the moments automation can support.

Existing state

No reply to one standard test email sent to Feirm's "Contact us" address (sent 08:00 Mon 12 Oct 2026, checked 14:30 Tue 13 Oct). Tirlán replied in 1 h 36 and Agridirect in 1 h 58.[1][2]

One test email per firm: a snapshot, not proof of Feirm's normal service. Who monitors the inbox is to be confirmed with Feirm.

18 of 25

Existing state

Customer-journey "moments of truth" rated weak or missing in the project research. Only 2 were rated strong, both at delivery.[2]

Team judgement based on five buyer journeys, not a measurement of Feirm's customer base.

1 day

Proposal

The project recommends one named owner for the enquiry inbox, an automatic acknowledgement and a reply within one working day.[2]

A target set in the project documents, not a figure Feirm has adopted.

What the project research already points to

Own every enquiry and problem

Name an owner for the inbox, acknowledge automatically, give each after-sales problem a case number and a named owner.[2]

Prompt the second order

No reorder, dosing, after-sales or review flows were evidenced in Klaviyo; timed reminders from order history are the largest open opportunity.[1][2]

Make services easier to reach

Clearer services and prescription information, status updates by text or WhatsApp, and easier mobile ordering.[2]

04 · The opportunity

One connected layer, with people at the centre

A connected, AI-assisted customer service and workflow automation layer for Feirm.

n8n would orchestrate routine processes behind the scenes, while a carefully controlled AI assistant supports customer enquiries and the staff who answer them.

Assist, don't replace

Feirm's people, agricultural expertise, veterinary advice and customer relationships are what make it different. The aim is a faster, more consistent team, not fewer conversations.

Start small, with staff approving

Begin with acknowledgement, categorisation, routing and drafts. Automatic substantive answers are optional and only considered after testing.

Prove the plumbing first

Nothing is assumed to connect to the Laravel shop or Herbst ERP until their APIs, permissions and data-access terms are confirmed.

05 · Technology landscape

Six building blocks, in plain English

Open any block for what it does, why Feirm might use it, what it depends on and what could go wrong.

n8n workflow automationThe orchestrator that links steps together

What it is

A visual tool for building automated workflows: something happens (a trigger such as a new email, a web request or a schedule), then a chain of steps runs. It can be used hosted by n8n or on your own servers.[6][3]

Why Feirm might use it

One place to connect inbox, staff notifications, AI drafting and Klaviyo, with workflows that staff can see. It supports a pause-for-approval step and error workflows, so failures are noticed.[8][9][11]

What it depends on

Access to each system (inbox, Klaviyo, and, if possible, shop and ERP), a licence model that fits who builds and hosts it, and someone to monitor it.[4]

What could go wrong

Unmonitored workflows failing silently; credentials with too much access; paying for more executions than expected. The docs reviewed do not label the AI and approval features beta, but their status should be re-checked at build time.

External research To verify Not plug-and-play with Feirm's systems until their APIs are confirmed.

AI customer enquiry assistantA website helper that answers from approved content

What it is

A chat on the website that can answer common questions about delivery, ordering, accounts, membership and prescription process, help customers find the right product category, capture an enquiry and pass it to staff.

Why Feirm might use it

Faster answers outside phone hours (the phone line closes at 5pm[1]) and staff who receive the conversation, so customers do not repeat themselves.

How it could be built

n8n has a Chat Trigger that can host a chat page or be embedded on a site, with options for authentication and allowed origins. Every message runs the workflow and uses one execution from the plan allowance.[10] A dedicated chatbot platform is the alternative; a hybrid is compared later.

What could go wrong

Wrong or out-of-date answers, prompt injection through customer messages, and over-reach into advice that belongs to a vet or pharmacist. It must not diagnose animals, prescribe, calculate doses or override professional advice.

Proposal Risk Phase 2 only, after the inbox workflow has proved itself.

Approved knowledge base (RAG)Grounding answers in Feirm's own material

What it is

"Retrieval-augmented generation" means the assistant first looks up passages in a controlled set of Feirm documents, then writes its answer from them. Behind this sits a vector store: a database that stores text as numbers so it can search by meaning.[12]

What would go in

Approved product descriptions, delivery and returns policies, membership information, prescription process and status guidance, FAQs, contact details and escalation routes.

Keep it simple first

A small, well-owned set of documents is easier to keep correct than a large one. A complex vector database should be recommended only if the use case justifies it.

Governance

Every document needs an owner, a review date and an approval route; answers should show their source; outdated items need a removal process; access should be limited by role.

Proposal To verify Who owns and approves the content is a decision for Feirm.

Enquiry inbox automationAcknowledge, categorise, route, draft, escalate

What it could do

Detect a new enquiry in an approved inbox; categorise it (orders, delivery, product information, prescriptions, accounts, membership, complaints, other); judge urgency; send an approved acknowledgement; notify the right team; prepare a draft reply for staff; remind or escalate if it goes unanswered; log response times.

Why it comes first

It addresses the clearest finding in the project research and keeps a person in control of every reply sent.[1]

How reminders work

n8n's Wait node can pause a workflow and resume after a time, at a set time, or when something happens, with a time limit that forces it to carry on. That is the basis for overdue reminders.[11]

What could go wrong

Misrouted or miscategorised enquiries, drafts that sound right but are wrong, and an acknowledgement that promises a response time nobody can meet.

Pilot Auto-sending substantive answers stays off until testing supports it.

Reorder and follow-up automationHelpful reminders at the right time

What it could do

Reorder reminders by product type and usual interval, after-sales check-ins, membership information after a first order, review requests, and relevant service reminders.

What Klaviyo needs

Klaviyo's predicted "expected date of next order" needs an ecommerce integration or API-sent orders, at least 180 days of order history with orders in the last 30 days, at least 500 customers who have ordered, and some who have ordered three or more times.[14] Whether Feirm's data meets this is unknown.

Consent and rules

In Ireland, electronic direct marketing generally needs the recipient's clear, affirmative consent, with a narrow exception for existing customers that has four conditions.[17] Preferences, suppression and frequency limits must be designed in.

What could go wrong

Reminders based on incomplete data, messages to people who opted out, or reminders for the wrong product or interval.

To verify Do not assume ERP or shop data is already synchronised into Klaviyo. Phase 3.

Optional later channelsWhatsApp Business, voice, reporting

WhatsApp Business

The project research already identifies WhatsApp as a natural channel for Feirm.[1] On the WhatsApp Business Platform, charges are per delivered message by category, replies within the 24-hour customer-service window are free, and rates vary by market; the page's rate calculator was not readable in this review.[20]

AI voice agents and predictive reporting

Possible later options for phone enquiries and for service and repeat-purchase reporting. They are not needed for the core proposal and no evidence is presented for them here.

Proposal Later-stage only; not a prerequisite for the pilot.

06 · How it would work

Three workflows, step by step

Select a step to see what happens and who does it. These are design sketches, not running systems.

A farmer asks a question on the website

  1. A farmer opens the Feirm websiteCustomer

    They see an offer of help with common questions, clearly labelled as an automated assistant, with a visible way to reach a person.

  2. The assistant offers help with common questionsn8n automation

    The chat passes each message to the workflow. Nothing is sent to Feirm's shop or ERP at this stage.

  3. It looks up approved Feirm information onlyAI modelKnowledge base

    Answers are drawn from the approved set: policies, FAQs, membership and prescription process, contact routes. If it is not in the set, the assistant does not guess.

  4. It answers and shows where the answer came fromAI model

    Where possible the reply names the source document, so staff and customers can check it.

  5. Uncertain, sensitive or out-of-scope? Offer a personAI modelHuman checkpoint

    Low confidence, a request for a person, or any topic touching animal health, medication, dosing or prescriptions triggers a safe response and a handoff. The assistant never diagnoses, prescribes or calculates doses.

  6. The enquiry is captured and routedn8n automation

    With the customer's agreement, name, contact details and enquiry category are recorded and sent to the right team through an approved workflow.

  7. Staff receive the whole conversationStaff member

    The original conversation and context travel with the enquiry, so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.

A customer email arrives

  1. A new customer email arrivesCustomer

    It lands in the approved enquiry inbox.

  2. n8n notices itn8n automationTo verify

    Needs a secure, approved connection to the shared inbox. Whether that is possible is a discovery question.

  3. AI reads it, classifies it and pulls out key detailsAI model

    Categories: orders, delivery, product information, prescriptions, accounts, membership, complaints, other. It also flags urgency and extracts details such as an order reference.

  4. An acknowledgement goes outn8n automation

    Sent from an approved template. It should promise only what staff can deliver, such as the project's suggested one-working-day reply.[2]

  5. The right person is notifiedn8n automation

    The enquiry is assigned or flagged to the relevant staff member or team, with a case reference.

  6. A draft reply is prepared for approvalAI modelStaff memberHuman checkpoint

    A person reviews, edits and sends. Nothing substantive is auto-sent in the pilot.

  7. Overdue? A reminder or escalation firesn8n automation

    If nobody has picked it up within the agreed time, the workflow reminds the owner or escalates. n8n's Wait node supports time-limited pauses.[11]

  8. The enquiry is logged for reportingn8n automation

    Times and outcomes feed the service measures later in this document.

A helpful reorder reminder

  1. An eligible order or customer event arrivesn8n automationTo verify

    It must come from a verified source. Whether shop or ERP order data can be delivered reliably is not yet confirmed.

  2. The workflow checks product, date, consent and suppressionn8n automation

    Is this a product that gets reordered? Has the customer agreed to be contacted by this channel? Are they on a suppression list? Has the frequency limit been reached?

  3. A reminder is scheduledn8n automationStaff knowledge

    Using an agreed interval per product, informed by staff knowledge of dosing seasons and, where available, Klaviyo's predictions.[14]

  4. The customer gets a relevant, helpful messageCustomer

    Through an approved channel, with a clear way to opt out and a link or phone number to reorder.

  5. Results are measured where appropriaten8n automation

    Clicks, replies or repeat orders, only where tracking is reliable and lawful.

12 · Risk, privacy and governance

Design controls in from day one

A vendor saying it "supports privacy" does not make a solution compliant. Feirm must assess its own configuration, contracts, purposes, notices and data flows. This is not legal advice.

Data protection

  • Principles. Purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation, accuracy and accountability apply.[18]
  • Marketing. Electronic direct marketing generally needs clear, affirmative consent; the existing-customer exception has four conditions, including a chance to object and a first message within 12 months of the sale.[17]
  • Transparency about AI. The Commission says people should be made aware they are interacting with a machine, and the AI Act became applicable on 2 August 2026 with some exceptions. Confirm the precise duties that apply to Feirm.[19]
  • Assessment. The DPC notes data-protection impact assessments are sometimes mandatory and expects organisations to understand where personal data goes, to keep human review, and to be able to handle access and deletion requests.[16]

Vendors, residency and retention

  • Contracts. n8n lists a data processing agreement, subprocessors and a SOC 2 Type 2 report on its Trust Center; the contents were not opened in this review.[13]
  • AI provider terms. Retention varies by model and feature. For Anthropic's API, conversation content is not retained by default except for models that require 30-day retention, flagged content may be kept up to 2 years, and zero data retention is a separate arrangement. Other providers differ.[15]
  • Location. n8n Cloud's hosting location is not confirmed from the official documents reviewed; confirm in the contract. To verify
  • Retention and deletion. Set a schedule for chat logs, drafts and enquiry records before launch.

Security

  • Never in the browser. API keys stay in the automation layer; a website chat only calls a webhook. n8n's chat can restrict allowed origins and require authentication.[10]
  • Least privilege. Dedicated, narrowly-scoped credentials. In n8n, projects, SSO, external secrets, Git version control and log streaming are paid features, not in Community.[7]
  • Encryption, audit logs, incidents. Require encryption in transit and at rest where supported, keep audit logs, and agree an incident process. To verify
  • Failures are visible. Error workflows and execution review show what failed.[9]

AI quality and safety

  • Wrong or stale answers. Approved content only, source shown, a clear "I don't know", and a review of unanswered questions.
  • Prompt injection. Treat customer messages and documents as untrusted. Give the assistant a fixed, small list of things it can do, and require human approval for actions with side effects; n8n can pause a tool call for approval.[8]
  • Human review. Staff approve drafts in the pilot; escalation is always available; there is a way to switch the assistant off.

The assistant's hard boundary

It must not diagnose animals, prescribe treatments, calculate medication doses or override professional advice. On those topics it gives a safe response and routes the customer to a qualified Feirm staff member or the appropriate professional.

13 · Success measures

Measure first, then compare

No baselines exist yet and none are invented here. Open a measure for its definition. Baselines are to be measured in Phase 0.

01Time to first acknowledgement

Definition. Time from an enquiry arriving to the customer receiving any automated or human acknowledgement.

Caution. An acknowledgement is not an answer; report it separately.

02Time to first human response

Definition. Time from arrival to the first reply written or approved by a person.

Caution. Measure in working hours, and agree how weekends and holidays count.

03Correct categorisation

Definition. Share of sampled enquiries where the AI's category matches a staff reviewer's.

Caution. Needs a reviewed sample, not a guess.

04Correct team routing

Definition. Share of enquiries that reach the right team without being re-routed.

05AI responses escalated

Definition. Share of assistant conversations handed to a person.

Caution. Neither high nor low is automatically good; review the reasons.

06Staff acceptance / edit rate of drafts

Definition. How often staff send an AI draft unchanged, edit it, or discard it.

Caution. High acceptance may signal over-trust. Sample sent drafts for accuracy.

07Backlog and overdue count

Definition. Number of open enquiries, and number past the agreed response time, at a set time each day.

08Enquiry resolution time

Definition. Time from arrival to the enquiry being marked resolved.

09Customer satisfaction

Definition. A short post-interaction rating or comment from customers who agree to give one.

Caution. Response rates are usually small; read comments, not just scores.

10Assistant containment

Definition. Share of conversations the assistant finished with no handoff.

Caution. Never the only measure: a customer who gave up is "contained" too. Pair with satisfaction and answer accuracy.

11Reorder reminder engagement and repeat orders

Definition. Opens, clicks or replies to reminders, and repeat orders that can be reliably attributed.

Caution. Only where tracking and consent are sound; do not claim credit for orders that would have happened anyway.

  1. BaselineMeasure today's response, backlog and categories first.
  2. PilotRun Phase 1 with staff oversight.
  3. CompareCompare like with like, against the baseline.
  4. ReviewLook at errors and staff feedback, not only the numbers.
  5. DecideExpand, adjust or stop at the decision gate.

14 · Recommendation and decisions required

Start with discovery, then the inbox.

We recommend Feirm approves Phase 0 only: a short, low-cost discovery to confirm access, ownership, data rules and a baseline. The first live pilot would then be the staff-approved enquiry workflow, before any customer-facing AI.

The trade-offs: the full benefit depends on integrations that are not yet confirmed, AI quality needs ongoing human care, and a poor acknowledgement could be worse than none.
